PRODUCT SPEC · UPGRAD · YOURDEGREE

YDST Scholarship Test

A learner registers on the scholarship page, pays a one-time registration fee and takes a 60-minute test on Gradr. Their score unlocks a discount on first-semester tuition straight away. Every step is sent to OneCRM so upGrad counsellors can follow up. The fee, what it covers, the attempt limit and the score-to-discount levers are all set by admins (9.2). The full journey is in 3.1, and every CRM touch point is in 8.1.

0.Overview & stakeholders

At a glance

What
An online scholarship test for upGrad's online degree programmes. Learners register, pay ₹100 once, take the test on Gradr and get a tuition discount based on their score.
Fee
Set by admins in settings. Default ₹100, paid once and valid for life. Admins can instead charge per attempt or for a fixed number of days (9.2).
Attempts
Set by admins in settings. Default one attempt per calendar month, with no fee for retakes while the fee is valid (3.2).
Discount
One target score per discount, set by admins in settings. Defaults: 51+ → 50% off, 41+ → 30% off, 31+ → 15% off first-semester tuition. Below the lowest target, no discount; the learner can retake when their next attempt opens (3.3).
Configurable
Fee amount, what the fee covers, the attempt limit and the score-to-discount levers are all edited in the admin dashboard (9.2), with no code release. A change applies only from that moment: learners keep what they already paid for, and finished attempts keep the discount they earned (3.5).
Journey
Scholarship page → register with OTP → pay the fee → payment confirmed → redirect to Gradr → real-time result → discount activated (3.1).
CRM
The lead is created in OneCRM at registration and updated at every later step, so counsellors always see where the learner is (8).
Built on
YDST scholarship page (existing prototype), upGrad payment gateway, Gradr test platform, OneCRM, fee system, OTP/SMS.
Key risks
Gradr's result callback must be reliable and secure, or discounts can't be activated in real time (6, 7). Lead updates to OneCRM must never be lost (8.3).
Still to settle
Gradr and OneCRM API details, refund policy, and the code validity period (see the open questions in 11.1).

0.1Problem

Learners comparing online degrees often stall at the fee. Generic discounts cut revenue without telling us who is serious. A short test with a small fee filters for intent: people who pay ₹100 and sit a 60-minute test are much more likely to enrol.

The test also gives counsellors a real reason to call ("you scored 46 and unlocked 30% off") and a deadline for the learner to act, since each code is valid for 30 days.

2.User stories

2.1Learner

  • I want to know in seconds what the test is, what it costs and how much I can save.
  • I want to register and pay in under 2 minutes, without a long form.
  • I want to start the test right after paying, without logging in again.
  • I want my result and my discount the moment I finish.
  • If I don't qualify, I want to know when I can try again and that it costs nothing.

2.2Counsellor (upGrad sales)

  • I want every YDST learner as a lead in OneCRM as soon as they register.
  • I want the lead to show the payment status, score, discount and code, updated in real time.
  • I want to know who paid but didn't test, and whose code is about to expire, so I can call them.

2.3Business and finance team

  • I want every ₹100 payment reconciled against the gateway, with transaction IDs.
  • I want discounts applied only through valid codes, so no one gets a discount they didn't earn.
  • I want a dashboard of fees collected, scores and discounts issued (9).

2.4Marketing team

  • I want UTM source, medium and campaign kept on the lead, so I can see which channel brings learners who qualify and enrol.
  • I want to change page copy without breaking the discount rules, which come from configuration (3.3).

3.How it works

Four ideas hold the product together:

  1. Pay once, test monthly (by default). By default the fee (₹100) is a lifetime registration that unlocks one attempt every calendar month. Admins can change what the fee covers and the attempt limit (9.2).
  2. The score decides the discount. Each target score unlocks one discount. Admins set the levers; no one approves individual discounts, and there are no exceptions.
  3. Results are instant. Gradr sends the score back the moment the test is submitted, and the discount is active before the learner leaves the result page.
  4. The CRM always knows. The lead is created at registration and updated at every step (8.1).

3.2Rules

  • One learner per mobile number. The OTP-verified mobile is the unique ID. Email is collected but isn't unique.
  • The fee covers what settings say. Default Lifetime: a learner who has paid is never asked to pay again. With Each attempt, they pay before every attempt. With Fixed days, they pay again once the days run out. A refunded learner must pay again to test.
  • Attempt limit from settings. Default: 1 attempt per calendar month (IST). Periods are counted in IST; calendar weeks start on Monday. Only a submitted or void attempt counts towards the limit. A test that's started but not submitted, or that breaks because of a Gradr error, doesn't count.
  • Paper follows the programme. Master's programmes get the advanced paper, bachelor's the foundation paper. The learner can change programme before each attempt; the paper changes with it.
  • Best active code wins. If a learner has more than one active code, the fee system applies the highest discount. A new attempt never cancels an earlier, better code.
  • Enrolling ends testing. Once a code is used to enrol, the learner can't take more attempts.

3.5When settings change

Admins can change the fee and the levers at any time (9.2). A change never reaches back into the past:

WhoWhat happens after a change
Learner who already paidKeeps what their payment covered when they paid. A lifetime payment stays lifetime even if the setting later becomes Each attempt or Fixed days. Never asked to pay the difference, never refunded part of it.
Attempt limit changesApplies to every learner from the next attempt. Attempts already taken in the current period count under the new limit. Example: limit goes from 1 to 2 per month; a learner who tested this month can test once more.
Learner with checkout openPays the amount on the order they opened. The order expires after 30 minutes; a new order uses the new fee.
Learner who hasn't paidSees and pays the new fee next time they load the page or open checkout.
Attempt already scoredKeeps its discount and code. Codes are never reissued or cancelled because levers changed.
Attempt in progressScored with the levers live when it was submitted.
Scholarship page copyFee, target scores and discounts update everywhere on the page within 5 minutes (4.2).
OneCRMNo bulk update. New touch points carry the fee paid and the settings version (8.2).

5.Payment

↔ How it connects
Starts after registration (4). On success, the learner goes to Gradr (6). Every outcome updates the lead (T2, T3).

5.1Checkout

  1. The backend creates an order for the fee live in settings at that moment (default ₹100, incl. GST), with learner ID, order ID and settings version. The amount is locked on the order. Order ID and amount are sent to OneCRM with T2.
  2. The gateway checkout opens on top of the page: UPI, cards, net banking and wallets.
  3. The learner pays. The page shows "Confirming your payment…" until the backend confirms.
  4. The backend marks the payment paid only when the gateway's signed webhook (or a server-side status check) says so. The browser redirect alone is never trusted.

5.2Outcomes

Gateway resultLearner seesSystem does
Success"Payment successful. Starting your test…" then redirect to Gradr (6.1)Stage → Paid. Receipt by email and SMS. T3 with transaction ID.
Failed"Payment didn't go through. No money was taken." Try againStage → Payment failed. T3 with failure reason.
Pending"Your bank is still confirming. We'll message you as soon as it's done."Checks status every 2 minutes for 30 minutes, then marks it failed. A late success still marks it paid.

5.3Refunds

  • Refunds are manual, by finance, only before the first attempt is submitted. To confirm
  • A refund sets the stage to Refunded and sends T7. Codes already issued stay valid. To confirm
⚙ Backend requirement
Payment handling must be idempotent: the same webhook delivered twice must not create two payments or two CRM updates. Store the gateway transaction ID against the order.

6.Test on Gradr

↔ How it connects
Starts after payment (5) or from a returning learner's Start test button. Ends with the result callback (7).

6.1Handoff to gradr.in

  • The backend asks Gradr to create an attempt for the learner and gets back a test URL. Gradr API to confirm
  • The learner is redirected to that URL with a short-lived signed token (valid 10 minutes, single use). No second login.
  • Sent to Gradr: attempt ID, learner ID, name, email, mobile, paper (Master's or Bachelor's), return URL.
  • If Gradr doesn't respond within 10 seconds, show "We couldn't start your test. Try again in a minute." The attempt isn't counted.

6.2Test format

SectionQuestionsNotes
Logical Reasoning201 mark each, no negative marking. 60 minutes in total. Same pattern for both papers; difficulty depends on the paper.
Quantitative Aptitude15
Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension15
Abstract Reasoning10
  • Proctoring: webcam on and full screen, run by Gradr. If Gradr flags the attempt, it comes back as Void (7.2).
  • Disconnects: Gradr resumes the same attempt if the learner returns within the 60 minutes. Gradr to confirm
  • Timeout: at 60 minutes Gradr submits the attempt automatically.

6.3Coming back from Gradr

After submitting, Gradr sends the learner to the return URL: the YDST result page (7.2). The result page never trusts scores in the URL. It shows only what the backend received from Gradr's callback (7.1).

7.Result and discount activation

7.1Real-time result

  1. On submit, Gradr calls the YDST result webhook with: attempt ID, total score out of 60, submitted time, proctoring status (clear or flagged). Payload to confirm
  2. The backend checks the signature, stores the result and works out the discount (3.3). This must take under 5 seconds.
  3. If the score hits a lever and the attempt isn't void, a scholarship code is issued (7.3) and T5 and T6 are sent to OneCRM.
  4. If no callback arrives within 2 minutes of the learner returning, the backend asks Gradr for the result directly.

7.2Result page

StateLearner sees
Waiting"Scoring your test…" with a spinner. Refreshes every 3 seconds for up to 2 minutes, then "Your result will reach you by SMS and email within 15 minutes."
50% / 30% / 15%Score out of 60, "You've unlocked <X>% off your first-semester tuition", the code with a copy button, expiry date, and "A counsellor will call you within 24 hours." Button: Explore universities.
Below 31Score out of 60, "You need <lowest target> to unlock <its discount>% off.", when the next attempt opens, and either "Retakes are free" or the retake fee, depending on settings.
Void"Your attempt was flagged by proctoring, so no scholarship can be given. You can try again from <date>."

7.3Scholarship code

  • Format: YD + paper (PG or UG) + 5 random characters, e.g. YDPG-K7Q2M. No ambiguous characters (0/O, 1/I).
  • Valid for 30 days from the attempt, for the programme tested, at any partner university. Not transferable.
  • Pushed to the fee system on issue, so the discount is applied when the learner pays the first-semester fee. Can't be combined with other offers.
  • Sent by SMS and email straight away.

8.3Delivery rules

  • Matching: the lead is found by mobile number. Never create a second lead for the same mobile.
  • Existing lead: if the mobile already exists in OneCRM from another source, update it and add YDST fields. Don't change its owner, except at T6 if it has none.
  • Order: each update carries the event time. OneCRM ignores an update older than the one it already has, so a late T2 can't overwrite T3.
  • Retries: updates go through a queue. Failures retry with back-off for 24 hours, then alert the on-call engineer. The learner's journey never waits for OneCRM.
  • Audit: every request and response is logged with the touch point and attempt ID.
⚙ Backend requirement
The learner's journey must never wait for or fail because of OneCRM. CRM delivery is asynchronous, idempotent per touch point and attempt, and retried until it succeeds.
